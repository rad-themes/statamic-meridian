body: |-
  A missed handoff is often a sign that no one knows who owns the decision. Adding more reminders rarely fixes that.

  Name the owner for each decision and describe when it should move to someone else. Bring repeated exceptions into a short team review.

  Accountability works best when people have the information and authority to act.
