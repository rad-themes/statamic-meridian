  A full queue often looks like a staffing problem. Sometimes it is. Sometimes good people spend too much time recovering from avoidable rework.

  Look at the path a request follows from start to finish. If ownership changes repeatedly or inputs arrive incomplete, improve that path before writing the job description.

  Then the next hire joins a team with room to do meaningful work.
